Marble Gauntlet — Terms of Service

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Marble Gauntlet is a private, single-operator tool. It publishes videos that its operator generates on his own computer to his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and provides no service to anyone else.

Who may use it

Only the owner of the TikTok account it is connected to. There is no sign-up, no account creation, and no way for a third party to use it.

What it does

  • Reads video files produced locally by the operator's own rendering software.
  • Uploads those files to the connected TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.
  • Reads the connected account's basic profile information (display name and follower count) so the operator can confirm the correct account is connected.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or transmit data belonging to any other person.
  • It does not scrape TikTok, browse other users' content, or automate engagement of any kind (no follows, likes, comments, or messages).
  • It does not resell, redistribute, or share access with anyone.

Content

Every video is generated by the operator's own software from assets he created or that are public domain or licensed for commercial use. No third-party video, music, or imagery is uploaded.

Compliance

Use of the tool is subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. If TikTok restricts or withdraws API access, the tool stops posting.

Liability

The tool is provided as-is to its own operator. There is no warranty and no third party relies on it.
