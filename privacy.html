Marble Gauntlet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Marble Gauntlet is a private tool used by one person to post his own videos to his own TikTok account. It has no users other than its operator and collects no data about anyone else.

What is stored, and where

  • An access token for the connected TikTok account. It is written to a file on the operator's own computer and is never sent anywhere except to TikTok's API.
  • The connected account's display name and follower count. Read on demand to confirm the right account is connected. Not retained.
  • A list of which video files have been posted, with timestamps. Kept locally so the same clip is not posted twice.

What is never collected

  • Any data about other TikTok users.
  • Contacts, location, device identifiers, or browsing activity.
  • Anything that would be shared with, sold to, or processed by a third party. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no external server: the tool runs entirely on one personal computer.

Retention and deletion

The token and the local log live only on the operator's computer and are deleted by deleting those files. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's security settings, which immediately makes the stored token useless.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and has no users other than its operator.
